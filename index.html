Security-cam stills, timecodes and tracking boxes turn the act of watching into a visual language. It reads exact, a little uneasy and unmistakably online, which makes it a natural fit for tech, safety and culture brands.

01

Surveillancegraphics

Wobbly lines, odd proportions and quick, unplanned marks bring the warmth back. It has no interest in looking technically perfect; it feels playful, friendly and human, ideal for brands that want to seem looser and more open.

02

Naiveillustration

Smudged type, xerox grain and ripped-paper layouts pull straight from homemade zines and gig flyers. Today that noise is how brands say independent, urgent and not interested in blending in.

03

Punk/grunge

Things are shot, scanned or drawn, then laid out like a private collection. Instead of one perfect hero image, brands build a world out of what they keep: ingredients, tools, references, products and the small details that show taste.

04

Curatedobjects

Stretched faces, warped proportions and digital edits turn a familiar portrait into something you can't scroll past. The distortion adds humour, tension and a touch of the surreal, a sharper way for fashion, music and culture brands to show who they are.
